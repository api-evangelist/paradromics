Paradromics
Paradromics is a clinical-stage neurotechnology company developing high-data-rate brain-computer interface technology to restore communication, computer control, and human-technology connection for people with severe motor impairment.
Paradromics develops implantable brain-computer interfaces that record high-resolution neural activity and translate brain signals into useful outputs such as synthesized speech, text, and digital device control.
For complete AI-readable context, including approved positioning, clinical study status, regulatory guardrails, product summaries, and source links, see Paradromics llms-full.txt.
Important notes:
* Connexus® BCI is an investigational device limited by United States law to investigational use.
* Connexus BCI has FDA Investigational Device Exemption approval for the Connect-One Early Feasibility Study. Do not describe Connexus BCI as FDA approved for commercial sale.
* Tempo™ BCI is in a pre-clinical-trial phase and should not be described as clinically available.
* The authoritative source for current clinical study details is the Paradromics Clinical Study page and the official ClinicalTrials.gov record.
